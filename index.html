FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about USD Core.

What is USD Core (USDCO)?+
USD Core (USDCO) is a BEP-20 token on BNB Smart Chain designed to maintain a 1:1 parity with the US Dollar. It is built for use as a digital payment instrument, enabling fast, borderless, and low-cost transactions.
How does the USD peg work?+
The USD peg is maintained operationally: each USDCO token is issued and redeemed at a 1:1 rate with the US Dollar. Liquidity on PancakeSwap supports market price stability.
Is the smart contract audited and verified?+
The USD Core smart contract is fully open-source and verified. The source code is publicly available on BscScan for anyone to review. An independent security audit is scheduled for Q1 2027.
Are there any fees for buying or selling USDCO?+
No. USD Core has 0% buy and sell tax. You only pay the standard BNB Smart Chain gas fee, which is typically a fraction of a cent.
Which wallets support USDCO?+
Any BNB Smart Chain-compatible wallet supports USDCO, including Trust Wallet, MetaMask, and Binance Wallet. Simply add the contract address to your wallet to see your balance.
Where can I buy USDCO?+
USDCO is available on PancakeSwap, OKX DEX, KyberSwap, and MEXC DEX+. You can also receive USDCO directly from our team for business payment purposes.
